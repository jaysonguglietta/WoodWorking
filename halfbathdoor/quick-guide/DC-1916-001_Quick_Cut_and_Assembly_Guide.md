# DC-1916-001 Quick Cut and Assembly Guide

This is the short shop-floor version of the full manual. It covers only lumber preparation, cutting, grooves, DF 500 mortises, panels, dry assembly, and glue-up.

## Before cutting

1. Measure the completed jamb. Confirm that 24 x 79 inches is the correct target slab size and record the required undercut.
2. Acclimate the quarter-sawn white oak to 6-9 percent moisture content.
3. Mark every part with its identifier, show face, reference face, reference edge, top, and bottom.
4. Make a test piece before every groove or Domino setup.

## Cutting sequence

1. Rough-cut every frame part oversize.
2. Joint one face and one edge.
3. Plane frame stock to about 1-7/16 inches. Sticker it and let it rest.
4. Final-plane all frame parts together to 1-3/8 inches.
5. Joint the reference edges, rip final widths, and crosscut rails to 15 inches.
6. Keep stiles long until the completed jamb has been confirmed; then crosscut to the verified target.
7. Mill panel blanks, glue edge joints where necessary, flatten, and cut the finished panel sizes.

## Panel grooves

- Groove size: 1/4 inch wide x 1/2 inch deep.
- Groove centerline: 11/16 inch from the show face.
- D-101C: groove the bottom edge only.
- D-101M, D-101D, and D-101E: groove both long edges.
- D-101F: groove the top edge only.
- D-101G: groove both long edges.
- D-101A and D-101B: use stopped grooves only within each panel opening. Do not run stile grooves through a Domino joint.

## DF 500 setups

All frame joints use a 10 mm cutter, 10 x 50 mm factory beech tenons, 25 mm plunge in each member, 17.5 mm fence height, and a 90-degree fence. Cut the first mortise in each joint tight and every additional mortise medium.

| Joint member | Tenons at each end | Centerlines from top/reference edge |
|---|---:|---|
| D-101C, D-101M, D-101E | 2 | 1-1/8 and 2-7/8 inches |
| D-101D lock rail | 3 | 1-1/8, 3-1/2, and 5-7/8 inches |
| D-101F bottom rail | 4 | 1-1/8, 4, 7-1/2, and 10-7/8 inches |

The muntin joints use a 6 mm cutter, two 6 x 40 mm factory beech tenons, 20 mm plunge in each member, and centerlines 15/16 and 2-1/16 inches from the left reference edge.

## Dry assembly

1. Insert dry-fit tenons and assemble the complete frame with all five panels.
2. Bring shoulders together with moderate hand or clamp pressure. Do not force a misaligned joint.
3. Confirm an overall target of 24 x 79 inches.
4. Measure both diagonals. Difference must not exceed 1/16 inch.
5. Check twist with winding sticks. Maximum twist is 1/32 inch.
6. Confirm every panel can move sideways in its groove.
7. Mark the clamp positions and assembly order before taking the door apart.

## Glue-up

1. Lay out all clamps, cauls, clean factory tenons, panels, glue, brushes, and a timer.
2. Rehearse the full assembly without glue.
3. Apply Type II PVA to the mortise walls and tenon faces. Do not fill the bottom of a mortise.
4. Assemble D-101E, D-101G, and D-101F first; then load the remaining rails and panels between the stiles.
5. Do not apply glue to any panel or panel groove.
6. Tighten clamps only enough to close the shoulders.
7. Recheck diagonals and flatness before applying final clamp pressure.
8. Remove accessible squeeze-out and allow the adhesive to reach its full specified cure.

## Final assembly checks

- All shoulders closed; no visible gap over 0.005 inch.
- Diagonals within 1/16 inch.
- Door flat within 1/32 inch.
- Five panels move independently.
- Stiles and rails remain 1-3/8 inches thick.
- Final perimeter fitting is performed against the completed jamb.
- Actual hinge leaves and mortise-lock hardware are measured before machining.

## Essential safety

Use guards, dust extraction, hearing protection, eye protection, and respiratory protection. Clamp work for Domino and router operations. Use push blocks for non-through dado cuts. Change the DF 500 mortise-width setting only while the motor is running and never during a plunge. Establish brass screw holes with matching steel screws and drive brass screws by hand.
