Complete CHK-RS-01 on all A-O, verify STOCK-A's current 72-3/4-inch usable length, STOCK-M's reported finished 3/4 x 8-1/4 x 99 envelope, and N/O moisture, grain, flatness, defects, and clear yield, then prove CHK-ST-01 on B/C plus CHK-ST-02 on I/K before releasing M/D/G/H panel crosscuts or E/F/J/L short-member production cuts.
2. Flatten one face, rest and recheck, plane the opposite face parallel, joint one edge, record actual dressed thickness/width/clear length, and stop at the controlled rough size until the next gate passes. Do not process every board to final size and measure only afterward.
3. Never surface a billet shorter or thinner than the machine manufacturer's safe minimum. Keep a longer mother piece or use a verified carrier/sled and guarded procedure when required.

## Balanced laminated-frame section

- Preglue: 5/16 face + 7/8 core + 5/16 face = 1-1/2.
- Finished: 1/4 face + 7/8 core + 1/4 face = 1-3/8.
- Remove 1/16 equally from each outer face after cure and rest. Machine grooves, Domino mortises, hinges, and lock work only afterward.
- Make every 5/16-inch face by staged thickness planing of a whole lane or mother billet. Ordinary rips divide board width; no board is divided through its thickness.
- All layers run with the member. No cross-grain layer, end splice, butt joint, or hidden short block is permitted.

## Fallback rules

- **Conservative Door Plan:** The baseline purchase quantity for the door is zero. Preserve every long board until the full dressed map, carrier-planing trials, signed core/face maps, and lamination coupons pass.
- **If Stile Face Fails:** Purchase one continuous long face board of adequate thickness and width; never end-splice a stile face.
- **If Stile Core Fails:** Purchase one clear long core board for the failed stile; never butt-, Domino-, or casually scarf-joint a stile core.
- **If Short Member Plan Fails:** Revise CM-05 first using clear finished-white-oak STOCK-N/O as short-member core reserve; revise FP-05 using clear uncommitted A-O reserve only where planing loss is justified; then purchase only the failed short-member layer if the signed map still cannot close.
- **If Panel Plan Fails:** Remap D-101H from M to clear STOCK-A first; use other door-only clear reserve only after the structural map is protected, then purchase replacement 4/4 panel stock.
- **Moulding:** Use only the separate S4S 4/4 and 6/4 purchase. Do not transfer A-O remnants to moulding until the completed door, retained coupons, and repair reserve are released.

Do not rough-length B, C, I, or K before the full-length stile gates pass. Keep N/O full unless a signed short-core fallback is released. Do not transfer A-O remnants to moulding before the completed door, coupons, and repair reserve are released.
