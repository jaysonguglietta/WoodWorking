# DF 500 Joint-Family Engineering

All frame mortises are cut only after the balanced lamination cures, rests, and is surfaced to 1/4 + 7/8 + 1/4. Reference the show face with a 17.5 mm fence height and 90-degree fence. Every sectioned production-family coupon must retain at least 0.200 inch of actual core wood from the cutter envelope to either face glue line. Frame joints use a tight first mortise and medium followers on both members. J-11/J-12 use two tight mortises in D-101G and a tight/medium pair in the mating rail. Stile grooves and the two muntin-adjacent rail grooves stop outside their joint zones.

## J-01 - Top rail to hinge stile

- Members: D-101C to D-101A. Cutter: 10 mm. Factory beech tenon: 10 x 50 mm. Quantity: 2.
- Plunge: 25 mm in each member. Part A centerlines from its marked reference edge: 1.25, 2.75 inches.
- Part B centerlines from the finished top of the stile: 1.25, 2.75 inches.
- Strategy: first mortise tight, 1 follower mortise(s) medium on both members. Primary rationale: face alignment and racking resistance.
- Groove analysis: mating stile groove is stopped; groove-adjacent rail mortises retain at least 0.150 inch actual web.
- Test gate: TP-01 must close by hand pressure, remain flush within 0.010 inch, and show no breakout, merged mortises, or groove intersection.
