# DF 500 Joint-Family Engineering

All frame mortises are cut from the show-face reference with a 17.5 mm fence height and 90-degree fence. The first centerline in each joint is tight; followers are medium. Stile grooves stop outside every joint zone.

## J-01 - Top rail to hinge stile

- Members: D-101C to D-101A. Cutter: 10 mm. Factory beech tenon: 10 x 50 mm. Quantity: 2.
- Plunge: 25 mm in each member. Centerlines from the marked top/left reference edge: 1.125, 2.875 inches.
- Strategy: first mortise tight, 1 follower mortise(s) medium. Primary rationale: face alignment and racking resistance.
- Groove analysis: mating stile groove is stopped; rail/muntin centerlines retain positive wood web to the 1/2-inch-deep panel groove.
- Test gate: TP-01 must close by hand pressure, remain flush within 0.010 inch, and show no breakout, merged mortises, or groove intersection.
