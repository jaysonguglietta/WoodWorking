# Rough and Finished Cut Lists

The release CSV derives every fixed part from the specification. Rails finish at the 15-inch shoulder length because loose-tenon construction has zero integral-tenon end allowance. Stiles remain 1 inch long during initial milling and retain a small perimeter fitting reserve until the completed opening is measured.

Panel sizes include two 1/2-inch groove engagements, minus movement clearance. Never replace a calculated door component with a vague field length.
