# Buy List

The release CSV is the controlled procurement list. Purchase approximately 26 board feet of 6/4 frame stock and 8 board feet of 4/4 panel stock after inspecting width, color, ray figure, and moisture. The 50 percent frame allowance covers selection loss in narrow, high-grade quarter-sawn oak. Do not price-lock the manual; obtain dated local quotations.

Hardware, Domino tenons and cutters, fresh adhesive, finish, abrasives, jig plywood, and combustion-safe waste control are required before their operation begins.
