# Flush, Scrape, and Surface

Let the adhesive reach full cure. Remove squeeze-out with a scraper rather than washing oak pores with water. Use a finely set smoothing plane across frame joints only after checking grain direction; stop before changing the 1-3/8-inch perimeter reference. Scrape ray-flecked surfaces in raking light. Sand uniformly through P100 to P120. A joint more than 0.020 inch out of flush is an assembly defect to assess, not a reason to hollow the entire face.
