# DC-1916-001 Quick Cut and Assembly Guide

**Current authority:** Rev. G / LS-05 / RL-02. LS-05 supersedes LS-04; RL-02 supersedes RL-01. Every earlier stock map and rough-length schedule is obsolete.

This is the short shop-floor version of the full manual. It covers only lumber preparation, cutting, grooves, DF 500 mortises, panels, dry assembly, and glue-up.

## Before cutting

1. Confirm the completed finished jamb is 24 x 81 inches clear throughout, as recorded. The Rev. G fitted slab is a maximum 23-3/4 x 80-1/2 x 1-3/8 inches: 1/8-inch hinge-side, lock-side, and head reveals plus a 3/8-inch bottom gap.
2. Build the cured, surfaced prefit assembly to 23-7/8 x 80-5/8 inches, carrying 1/16 inch removable stock outside each final perimeter edge. If the rechecked jamb differs from 24 x 81 or cannot accept the specified reveals/gap, stop and re-engineer every affected rail, opening, panel, story-stick, and mortise coordinate before cutting.
3. Acclimate the quarter-sawn white oak to 6-9 percent moisture content.
4. Mark every part with its identifier, show face, reference face, reference edge, top, and bottom.
5. Make a test piece before every groove or Domino setup.
6. Read LS-05 and RL-02 and document CHK-RS-01, CHK-ST-01, CHK-ST-02, CHK-SM-01, CHK-P-01, CHK-LAM-01, and CHK-SEAM-01 before releasing lumber to production.

> **Superseded-history warning:** Every prior stile width, rail-shoulder length, panel size, stock map, and rough-length schedule is obsolete. Use only Rev. G / LS-05 / RL-02 values.

## LS-05 / RL-02 rough-sawn stock disposition

- **Surface condition is mixed:** STOCK-B-F and STOCK-H-K remain rough; STOCK-G is now S4S; STOCK-L has cleaned edges; and STOCK-M/N/O are reported finished. Every board still requires the applicable thickness, width, length, straightness, moisture, grain, color, and defect checks. A reported surface condition or dimension does not by itself prove usable yield.
- **A-O are door-only stock:** Reserve all fifteen boards for the door, same-layup coupons, setup pieces, substitutions, and future repair stock. Do not credit any A-O material to moulding.
- **N/O are uncut reserve:** STOCK-N and STOCK-O are each confirmed finished white oak at 1-3/4 x 11 x 74 inches. Record moisture, grain, flatness, defects, and clear yield before assigning them. At 74 inches they cannot replace an 81-inch continuous stile layer. Keep both full as preferred short-member core reserve.
- **No door-lumber purchase is released:** The stock-only map is conditional on the full-length stile, short-member, panel, lamination-coupon, and seam-map gates. If a long stile layer fails, buy only a replacement continuous board; never end-splice it.
- **No-resaw rule:** Do not slice a board through its thickness. Face lamellae are made by jointing one face, ordinary width-ripping where scheduled, and thickness-planing the whole mother billet to 5/16 inch in staged, alternating passes. Sticker and rest at intermediate thickness; use a flat planer carrier or sled if the billet cannot register safely on the planer bed.
- **Balanced frame section:** Prepare a 5/16-inch show-face lamella, a 7/8-inch core, and a 5/16-inch back-face lamella. Press both faces simultaneously to a 1-1/2-inch glue blank, cure and rest, then remove 1/16 inch equally from each outer face to finish at 1/4 + 7/8 + 1/4 = 1-3/8 inches.
- **Critical long members:** D-101A uses a continuous STOCK-B show face, one STOCK-K lane as the 7/8-inch core, and K’s parallel lane as the back face. D-101B uses a continuous STOCK-C show face, one STOCK-I lane as the core, and I’s parallel lane as the back face. Before ripping, each I/K board must prove at least 8-7/8 inches of clear jointed width for two 4-3/8-inch lanes plus a 1/8-inch kerf. Ordinary-rip the lanes; do not slice either lane through its thickness. The show/core/back layer order is B/K/K for D-101A and C/I/I for D-101B. No stile layer may contain an end joint.
- **Short-member pool:** Prepare rail and muntin cores from STOCK-J/L. Draw the initial face pool from clear STOCK-D/H tails, C/J/L offcuts, and at least five clear 16-1/4-inch billets proven across the combined knot-mapped STOCK-E/F inventory. After D-101H passes CHK-P-01, the protected STOCK-M tail may provide up to two 16-1/4-inch face mother billets. Keep STOCK-A intact as the door-only fallback reserve.
- **Mapped stave-built rails:** D-101D and D-101F use staggered core/show/back seams controlled by CHK-SEAM-01. D-101A/B/C/M/E/G retain one-piece cores.
- **Panels are conditional:** STOCK-M is the primary D-101H source; STOCK-G/H/D carry the remaining panel map. STOCK-G is confirmed S4S at **15/16 x 4-1/2 x 75 inches**. Its thickness exceeds the 5/8-inch D-101J rough-panel minimum by 5/16 inch, and its four-stave width/length pattern leaves a 6-1/4-inch planning tail after the shared reserve. Thickness, width, and length pass; do not crosscut until CHK-P-01 proves flatness, moisture, grain, defects, glue-edge cleanup, and four clear billet zones. STOCK-A is an intact fallback only.

## Cutting sequence

1. Complete CHK-RS-01 with every A-O board still full length. Map defects, clear squared-end limits, minimum thickness/width, moisture, bow, cup, twist, pith, and runout. Record STOCK-G separately as **S4S: 15/16 x 4-1/2 x 75 inches**. Its thickness, width, and length fit D-101J, but do not crosscut it until CHK-P-01 proves all four clear billet zones. Verify STOCK-M despite its reported finished condition; for confirmed finished-white-oak N/O, record moisture, grain, flatness, defects, and clear yield.
2. Prove one continuous 5/16 x 4-3/8 x 81-1/8 show face from STOCK-B for D-101A and one from STOCK-C for D-101B. Joint one datum face while each board is whole, thickness-plane the complete billet in staged alternating passes, sticker/rest it before the last passes, and use a flat carrier if required. Do not resaw.
3. Joint one face and one edge of STOCK-K and STOCK-I while each board is whole. Prove 8-7/8 inches of clear jointed width, then ordinary-rip each into two continuous 4-3/8-inch lanes with the controlled 1/8-inch kerf. From K release one 7/8 x 4-3/8 x 81 D-101A core and one 5/16 x 4-3/8 x 81-1/8 back face; from I release the corresponding D-101B core and back face. Stage, sticker, rest, and carrier-plane the 5/16 lanes as required. The 1-inch B/C rough stock is not a 7/8-inch core source.
4. Sign the J/L core nesting and D/H-tail, C/J/L-offcut, and knot-mapped E/F face-pool map under CHK-SM-01. The combined E/F map must prove at least five clear 16-1/4-inch billets before short-member production starts. Keep A intact as reserve. Release up to two 16-1/4-inch face mother billets from M's protected tail only after D-101H passes CHK-P-01. Keep every layer or stave continuous with its member length.
5. Prepare core and face sheets separately. For every pressing family, make and destructively inspect a same-layup coupon under CHK-LAM-01.
6. Dry-stack D-101A as B show face / K core / K back face and D-101B as C show face / I core / I back face. Verify grain direction, glue-ready contact, and 4-3/8-inch blank width. Apply adhesive to the prepared long-grain faces, press the 5/16 + 7/8 + 5/16 stack with both faces applied simultaneously against a flat platen, and prevent layer slip with registered cauls.
7. Cure and rest with air to both faces. Remove exactly 1/16 inch from each outside face in alternating light passes, then final-machine the balanced blank to 1-3/8 inch without cutting into the 1/4-inch finished skins.
8. Transfer CHK-SEAM-01 before Domino work: D-101D core/show/back seams remain 2-1/4, 4-3/8, and 4-7/8 inches from its top. D-101F has one core seam at 5-3/4; show-face seams at 3, 6-1/2, and 9-1/2; and back-face seams at 2-1/2, 5, and 9 inches from its top. Do not move a Domino center to accommodate a seam.
9. Joint the reference edges, rip final widths, and crosscut rails to 15-1/4 inches only after preserving the 16-1/4-inch machining blanks. Finish both stiles at equal 4-1/4-inch widths. Keep the stile layers long through pressing; surface and trim the cured assembly to the 23-7/8 x 80-5/8 prefit rectangle before scribing the 23-3/4 x 80-1/2 final rectangle.
10. After CHK-P-01 passes, mill D-101H from three STOCK-M billets rough-crosscut at 16-1/16 inches, then mill the remaining panel glue-ups from STOCK-G/H/D. Keep STOCK-A intact unless a documented panel failure requires the fallback. Actual clear squared-end loss controls.

## Panel grooves

- Groove size: 1/4 inch wide +0.005/-0.000 x 1/2 inch deep +0.010/-0.000.
- Groove centerline: 11/16 inch from the show face.
- On the identified SawStop, use only a compatible 8-inch stacked dado with its dedicated dado brake cartridge and compatible insert; the exact saw manual controls. Use that setup only for conventionally fed, fully supported through grooves: D-101C bottom; D-101M both long edges; D-101D both long edges; D-101E top.
- Use a plunge router with an edge guide or captured jig and positive start/stop blocks for every stopped or segmented groove: D-101E bottom from 0 to 6-1/8 and 9-1/8 to 15-1/4 inches; D-101F top over those same spans; both D-101G long edges; and opening-only segments in D-101A/B. The 3-inch receiver footprint is 6-1/8 to 9-1/8 from the hinge-side rail shoulder.
- Never blind-plunge or lift a workpiece onto a rotating dado set. Do not run stile grooves through a Domino joint.

## DF 500 setups

All frame rail-to-stile joints use a 10 mm cutter, 10 x 50 mm factory beech tenons, 25 mm plunge in each member, 17.5 mm fence height, and a 90-degree fence. Cut the first mortise in each member tight and every follower medium.

The 17.5 mm fence keeps both the 10 mm and 6 mm cutter bands inside the 7/8-inch core. On sectioned same-layup samples, retain at least 0.200 inch of actual core wood between each DF cutter envelope and either face glue line. The centered 1/4-inch panel groove must also remain entirely inside the core. Rail-local and vertical stile center patterns remain as listed below; use only the Rev. G story stick.

The muntin joints use a 6 mm cutter, two 6 x 40 mm factory beech tenons, and 20 mm plunge in each member. On D-101G, locate the centerlines 1 and 2 inches from its left reference edge and cut both mortises tight. A medium follower in the 3-inch muntin would compromise the groove-side web. On D-101E and D-101F, preserve the solid 3-inch footprint from 6-1/8 to 9-1/8 inches from the hinge-side rail shoulder; cut the matching receiver centers at 7-1/8 and 8-1/8 inches, first tight and second medium.

Before cutting J-06 in the lock stile, map the physical mortise-lock body, faceplate, spindle, key/turn, escutcheon, fasteners, and every required machining opening. Apply the 1/4-inch clear-wood buffer only between that complete lock-machining envelope and J-06 or other structural joinery. Re-engineer J-06 before mortising if the buffered lock envelope intersects a Domino mortise, panel groove, structural shoulder, or structural fastener path. The faceplate recess, spindle/key openings, edge entry, and other intentional hardware face/edge openings are not subject to the 1/4-inch joinery buffer; instead, verify each one against the physical hardware and manufacturer's pattern, confirm that intended hardware shoulders and screw support remain, and reject any unintended breakthrough or merged cut.

In the cross-thickness view, keep the lock-body machining allowance at least 1/16 inch from each face glue line. Verify hinge-gain depth remains within the nominal 1/4-inch face lamella and that hinge screws penetrate sound core wood.

## Visual assembly sequence

1. Lay every cut component on the bench in final door order. Keep all show faces upward and confirm every identifier.
2. Dry-lay D-101A and D-101B outside the rails. Place D-101C, D-101M, D-101D, D-101E, and D-101F at their story-stick positions.
3. Build one lower module from D-101E, D-101G, and D-101F. Before either lower-panel tongue enters D-101G, place two approved 3/4-inch-long foam spacers in each D-101G side groove - four total. Capture D-101K and D-101L without panel glue, then close the E/G/F joints.
4. At D-101A, place two approved 3/4-inch-long foam spacers in each vertical groove segment that will receive D-101H, D-101J, D-101N, and D-101K. Seat D-101C, D-101M, and D-101D into A; slide H and J laterally from the open lock-stile end; then slide the one completed lower module leftward so the E/F tenons and K outer tongue enter A together. With E now seated, slide N through the D/E grooves until its outer tongue enters A. Do not install a second loose D-101E or D-101F.
5. Before a panel tongue enters D-101B, place two approved 3/4-inch-long foam spacers in each B-side groove segment for D-101H, D-101J, D-101N, and D-101L. Start B over every exposed rail tenon and panel tongue, keeping it parallel to A so all joints enter together.
6. Install alternating-face clamps at the rail locations. Close shoulders with moderate pressure, compare diagonals, check twist, and confirm that all five panels retain lateral movement with the spacers installed.

## Dry assembly

1. On paired production-thickness samples, first prove that shoulders close by hand with untouched, full-length factory tenons. Only after that gate passes, make the clearly marked rehearsal set from non-production tenons by minimally easing ribs and ends just enough for repeated hand removal; **never shorten a tenon**. Procure 60 factory 10 x 50 mm tenons and 12 factory 6 x 40 mm tenons so one untouched production set and one retained test set remain after setup and spares.
2. Assemble the complete frame with all five panels while the D-101E/G/F lower module is still unglued.
3. Bring shoulders together with moderate hand or clamp pressure. Do not force a misaligned joint.
4. Confirm the 23-7/8 x 80-5/8 prefit perimeter and the complete 80-1/2-inch final story-stick stack inside it. The scribed fitted-slab rectangle must not exceed 23-3/4 x 80-1/2.
5. Measure both diagonals. Difference must not exceed 1/16 inch.
6. Check twist with winding sticks. Maximum twist is 1/32 inch.
7. Confirm every panel can move sideways in its groove with all approved 3/4-inch-long foam spacers installed. Reject the spacer/sample combination if compression resists shoulder closure, bows a member, eliminates residual panel travel, or contaminates a finish/glue surface.
8. Mark the clamp positions and assembly order before taking the door apart.

## Glue-up

1. Use two stages only after the complete unglued-door dry fit passes: Stage A is the lower D-101E/D-101G/D-101F module with four 6 x 40 mm tenons; Stage B is the full frame with twenty-six 10 x 50 mm tenons.
2. Rehearse each stage with the relieved test tenons. Record shop temperature and elapsed time against only the selected Type II PVA manufacturer's **current published OPEN time** at that temperature; no differently labeled timing window qualifies. Time from first simulated adhesive contact through the final geometry and panel-movement check. Each rehearsal must finish at or below 75 percent of published open time.
3. Stop if either final rehearsal gate exceeds 0.75 times current published open time. Correct the sequence, add help, or select a suitable longer-open-time Type II PVA before continuing.
4. Stage A: while both D-101G side grooves are accessible, place two approved 3/4-inch-long foam spacers in each groove - four total - before the D-101K or D-101L inner tongue enters. Start the live timer at first adhesive contact; glue both mating mortise walls and every face of the four clean muntin tenons, capture K/L dry, and clamp the E/G/F module square. Record clamp complete as a checkpoint only and keep the timer running through the final square/equal-opening and K/L movement check; pass only when that final gate is <=75 percent of current published open time. Then allow full cure.
5. After Stage A reaches full cure, assemble the complete door a second time with relieved test tenons. Confirm shoulders, panel movement, the 23-7/8 x 80-5/8 prefit perimeter, the Rev. G story-stick stack, diagonals, and twist; then time a no-adhesive Stage B rehearsal.
6. Stage B: lay out at least five rail-location clamps, padded cauls, two tapes, winding sticks, panels, brushes, timer, and all twenty-six clean frame tenons.
7. Start the Stage B live timer at first adhesive contact. Apply glue to both mating mortise walls and every face of the frame tenons. Do not fill the bottom of a mortise.
8. Before any panel or module tongue enters D-101A, place two approved 3/4-inch-long foam spacers in each A-side groove segment for H, J, N, and K. Seat C, M, and D into A; load H and J; then load the one cured E/G/F/K/L lower module so its E/F tenons and K tongue enter A together. With E seated, slide N through D/E until its outer tongue enters A. Do not add loose duplicate E/F parts.
9. Before any panel tongue enters D-101B, place two approved 3/4-inch-long foam spacers in each B-side groove segment for H, J, N, and L. Bring B over all five rail ends and the four exposed panel tongues in parallel.
10. Tighten clamps only enough to close the shoulders. Record clamp complete as a checkpoint only; the Stage B live timer continues.
11. Apply only the final holding pressure needed to maintain closed shoulders, then recheck diagonals, twist, and free lateral movement of all five panels. Record the final geometry/panel-movement time; Stage B passes only when it is <=75 percent of current published open time.
12. Remove accessible squeeze-out and allow the adhesive to reach its full specified cure.

## Final assembly checks

- All shoulders closed; no visible gap over 0.005 inch.
- Diagonals within 1/16 inch.
- Twist measured with winding sticks within 1/32 inch.
- Five panels move independently.
- All 20 approved 3/4-inch-long foam spacers prevent rattle without joint stress or finish contamination.
- Stiles and rails remain 1-3/8 inches thick.
- Finished frame sections remain balanced at nominal 1/4 + 7/8 + 1/4; no void, slip, glue-line separation, or exposed under-thickness face.
- The installed jamb is 24 x 81 inches clear. The cured surfaced prefit assembly is 23-7/8 x 80-5/8; the finished fitted slab is no larger than 23-3/4 x 80-1/2 x 1-3/8, producing 1/8-inch side/head reveals and a 3/8-inch bottom gap.
- Actual hinge leaves and mortise-lock hardware are measured before machining.

## Production tolerances

- Frame thickness 1-3/8 inch +/-0.010; paired rails within 0.005 inch; reference edges square within 0.003 inch per inch.
- Preglue frame layers are 5/16 + 7/8 + 5/16; face layers are produced by staged thickness planing, never resawing. Remove 1/16 inch equally from both outside faces to finish at nominal 1/4 + 7/8 + 1/4. Same-layup coupons must fail in wood rather than at an adhesive line.
- D-101D core/show/back seams are 2-1/4, 4-3/8, and 4-7/8 inches from the finished top. D-101F has a core seam at 5-3/4, show seams at 3, 6-1/2, and 9-1/2, and back seams at 2-1/2, 5, and 9 inches. Seam location is +/-1/32 inch, core-to-face offset is at least 1/2 inch, and the released mortise-planform clearance must remain intact.
- Groove width is 0.250 +0.005/-0.000; depth is 0.500 +0.010/-0.000; opposed groove centerline mismatch is no more than 0.010 inch.
- The approved panel spacer is nominally 0.265 x 0.265 x 0.750 inch; 0.750 inch is longitudinal length, and the 0.265-inch foam cross-section compresses into the unchanged 0.250-inch-wide groove.
- Section production-equivalent samples: measure **every relevant standard-rail tight and medium mortise-to-groove web** at 0.150 inch minimum; D-101G groove-to-mortise webs at 0.100 inch minimum; D-101G inter-mortise webs at 0.220 inch minimum; and E/F rail-receiver inter-mortise webs at 0.100 inch minimum.
- Every joint-family test shoulder closes by hand, faces remain flush within 0.010 inch, and no mortise intersects a groove, merges with another mortise, breaks through a face, approaches a face glue line closer than 0.200 inch, or enters a released stave seam.

## Essential safety

Use guards, dust extraction, hearing protection, eye protection, and respiratory protection. Clamp work for Domino and router operations. Use push blocks for non-through dado cuts. Change the DF 500 mortise-width setting only while the motor is running and never during a plunge. Establish brass screw holes with matching steel screws and drive brass screws by hand.
