# DC-1916-001 Quick Cut and Assembly Guide

This is the short shop-floor version of the full manual. It covers only lumber preparation, cutting, grooves, DF 500 mortises, panels, dry assembly, and glue-up.

## Before cutting

1. Measure the completed jamb. Confirm that 24 x 79 inches is the correct target slab size and record the required undercut.
2. If the verified slab width is not exactly 24 inches, stop. Recalculate and revalidate rail lengths, opening widths, and panel widths before crosscutting any rail.
3. Acclimate the quarter-sawn white oak to 6-9 percent moisture content.
4. Mark every part with its identifier, show face, reference face, reference edge, top, and bottom.
5. Make a test piece before every groove or Domino setup.

## Cutting sequence

1. Rough-cut every frame part oversize.
2. Joint one face and one edge.
3. Plane frame stock to about 1-7/16 inches. Sticker it and let it rest.
4. Final-plane all frame parts together to 1-3/8 inches.
5. Joint the reference edges, rip final widths, and crosscut rails to 15 inches.
6. Keep stiles long until the completed jamb has been confirmed; then crosscut to the verified target.
7. Mill panel blanks, glue edge joints where necessary, flatten, and cut the finished panel sizes.

## Panel grooves

- Groove size: 1/4 inch wide x 1/2 inch deep.
- Groove centerline: 11/16 inch from the show face.
- D-101C: groove the bottom edge only.
- D-101M, D-101D, and D-101E: groove both long edges.
- D-101F: groove the top edge only.
- D-101G: groove both long edges.
- D-101A and D-101B: use stopped grooves only within each panel opening. Do not run stile grooves through a Domino joint.

## DF 500 setups

All frame joints use a 10 mm cutter, 10 x 50 mm factory beech tenons, 25 mm plunge in each member, 17.5 mm fence height, and a 90-degree fence. Cut the first mortise in each joint tight and every additional mortise medium.

| Joint member | Tenons at each end | Centerlines from top/reference edge |
|---|---:|---|
| D-101C, D-101M, D-101E | 2 | 1-1/8 and 2-7/8 inches |
| D-101D lock rail | 3 | 1-1/8, 3-1/2, and 5-7/8 inches |
| D-101F bottom rail | 4 | 1-1/8, 4, 7-1/2, and 10-7/8 inches |

The muntin joints use a 6 mm cutter, two 6 x 40 mm factory beech tenons, and 20 mm plunge in each member. On D-101G, locate the centerlines 15/16 and 2-1/16 inches from its left reference edge. On D-101E and D-101F, center the 3-inch muntin footprint from 6 to 9 inches from the hinge-side rail shoulder; the matching centerlines are 6-15/16 and 8-1/16 inches from that shoulder.

## Visual assembly sequence

1. Lay every cut component on the bench in final door order. Keep all show faces upward and confirm every identifier.
2. Dry-lay D-101A and D-101B outside the rails. Place D-101C, D-101M, D-101D, D-101E, and D-101F at their story-stick positions.
3. Build the lower module from D-101E, D-101G, and D-101F. Capture D-101K and D-101L in their grooves without panel glue.
4. Seat all rail tenons into D-101A. Slide D-101H, D-101J, and D-101N laterally from the open lock-stile end, followed by the completed lower module.
5. Start D-101B over every exposed rail tenon. Keep it parallel to D-101A so all joints enter together.
6. Install alternating-face clamps at the rail locations. Close shoulders with moderate pressure, compare diagonals, check twist, and confirm panel movement.

## Dry assembly

1. Use clearly marked test tenons that have been shortened slightly and edge-sanded for disassembly. Keep all 30 untouched factory tenons reserved for glue-up.
2. Assemble the complete frame with all five panels.
3. Bring shoulders together with moderate hand or clamp pressure. Do not force a misaligned joint.
4. Confirm an overall target of 24 x 79 inches.
5. Measure both diagonals. Difference must not exceed 1/16 inch.
6. Check twist with winding sticks. Maximum twist is 1/32 inch.
7. Confirm every panel can move sideways in its groove.
8. Mark the clamp positions and assembly order before taking the door apart.

## Glue-up

1. Use two stages: Stage A is the lower D-101E/D-101G/D-101F module with four 6 x 40 mm tenons; Stage B is the full frame with twenty-six 10 x 50 mm tenons.
2. Rehearse each stage with the relieved test tenons. Record the shop temperature and elapsed time. Add a 25 percent handling margin and compare the result with the selected Type II PVA manufacturer's current published open or assembly time.
3. Stop if rehearsal time plus margin exceeds the adhesive limit. Correct the sequence, add help, or select a suitable longer-open-time Type II PVA before continuing.
4. Stage A: apply glue to both mating mortise walls and every face of the four clean muntin tenons. Capture D-101K and D-101L dry with their space balls, clamp the module square, and allow it to reach full cure.
5. Stage B: lay out at least five rail-location clamps, padded cauls, two tapes, winding sticks, panels, brushes, timer, and all twenty-six clean frame tenons.
6. Apply glue to both mating mortise walls and every face of the frame tenons. Do not fill the bottom of a mortise.
7. Load the cured lower module, remaining rails, and panels between the stiles. Do not apply glue to any panel tongue or groove.
8. Tighten clamps only enough to close the shoulders.
9. Recheck diagonals and twist with winding sticks before applying final clamp pressure.
10. Remove accessible squeeze-out and allow the adhesive to reach its full specified cure.

## Final assembly checks

- All shoulders closed; no visible gap over 0.005 inch.
- Diagonals within 1/16 inch.
- Twist measured with winding sticks within 1/32 inch.
- Five panels move independently.
- Stiles and rails remain 1-3/8 inches thick.
- Final perimeter fitting is performed against the completed jamb.
- Actual hinge leaves and mortise-lock hardware are measured before machining.

## Production tolerances

- Frame thickness 1-3/8 inch +/-0.010; paired rails within 0.005 inch; reference edges square within 0.003 inch per inch.
- Groove width 0.250 inch +0.005/-0.000; groove depth 0.500 inch +/-0.010; opposed groove centerline mismatch no more than 0.010 inch.
- Every joint-family test shoulder closes by hand, faces remain flush within 0.010 inch, and no mortise intersects a groove, merges with another mortise, or breaks through a face.

## Essential safety

Use guards, dust extraction, hearing protection, eye protection, and respiratory protection. Clamp work for Domino and router operations. Use push blocks for non-through dado cuts. Change the DF 500 mortise-width setting only while the motor is running and never during a plunge. Establish brass screw holes with matching steel screws and drive brass screws by hand.
