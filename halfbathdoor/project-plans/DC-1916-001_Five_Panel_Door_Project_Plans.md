# DC-1916-001 Five-Panel White Oak Door — Project Plans

**Control:** Rev. G / LS-05  
**Confirmed jamb opening:** 24 x 81 inches  
**Finished fitted slab:** 23-3/4 x 80-1/2 x 1-3/8 inches  
**Prefit assembly:** 23-7/8 x 80-5/8 inches  
**Installed gaps:** 1/8 inch at head and both sides; 3/8-inch bottom gap

This source outlines the concise, image-led project-plans booklet. The booklet
uses the restrained cadence of a premium furniture plan—one construction idea
per widescreen page—while retaining the complete dimensional controls of the
door package.

## Critical build controls

- Dimensions are written in **THICKNESS x WIDTH x LENGTH** order.
- Rough stock is surveyed before crosscutting. Boards B, C, I, and K remain full
  length until continuous stile yield is proven.
- Board G is confirmed S4S at **15/16 x 4-1/2 x 75 inches**. Its thickness
  exceeds the 5/8-inch D-101J rough-panel minimum by 5/16 inch. The four-stave
  width/length plan leaves a 6-1/4-inch planning tail after the shared reserve.
  Thickness, width, and length pass; CHK-P-01 still controls crosscut release.
- Rev. G is a no-resaw design. Face lamellae are prepared by staged planing from
  whole-thickness boards or billets.
- Frame blanks are pressed at 5/16 + 7/8 + 5/16 inches, then surfaced equally
  from both faces to 1/4 + 7/8 + 1/4 inches.
- Frame grooves are 1/4 inch wide x 1/2 inch deep. Panel tongues project
  7/16 inch and finish 7/32 inch thick.
- Use 3/4-inch-long foam panel spacers; do not glue the floating panels.
- The cured assembly is squared and surfaced to 23-7/8 x 80-5/8 inches before
  final fitting to 23-3/4 x 80-1/2 x 1-3/8 inches.
